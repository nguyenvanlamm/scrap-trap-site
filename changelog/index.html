02 / What is in the build

The room is
already moving.

Core escape

  • 12 levels across three acts: Scrap Yard, Maintenance Halls and The Grinder.
  • 13 trap types built around learning from a fired trap.
  • Power-core escape: pulling the core starts the crusher chase toward the true exit.

Battery and challenge systems

  • Scan and EMP skills use the battery economy: one battery to scan, two to EMP.
  • Persistent discoveries keep fired or scanned trap marks after death.
  • Ranks and goals: S/A/B/C ranking plus no-death, no-skill and under-par challenges.

Full-game unlock

  • Levels 1–3 are free; the remaining levels unlock with one optional, one-time in-app purchase.
  • Restore purchase recovers the unlock from your store account after reinstalling.
  • Kept on reset: clearing progress does not remove the unlock.

Sound and settings

  • Bundled audio with music and sound-effect controls.
  • Persisted settings for music, SFX, haptics, reduced motion and left-handed controls.
