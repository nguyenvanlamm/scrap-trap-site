01 / Read first

What this
policy covers.

This policy covers the current Scrap Trap mobile game, which is in development, this website and the public GitHub support forms managed by the Scrap Trap maintainers.

No account needed to play

Your progress and settings are stored locally. The game does not send gameplay data to servers operated by the Scrap Trap maintainers.

02 / Inside the game

Progress stays
close to the run.

The current game has no player accounts, analytics, advertising or tracking. It offers one optional in-app purchase, described below. It stores the following information on your device so you can continue playing and keep your preferences.

Stored by the appWhy it existsCurrent handling
Unlocked levels and recordsResume progress and show deaths, times, batteries and ranks.Local preferences/save data on the device.
Challenge goals and viewed hintsRemember challenge completion and one-time guidance.Local until progress is reset or the app is removed.
Accessibility choicesKeep reduced motion and left-handed controls consistent.Settings survive a progress reset.
Audio settingsRemember music, SFX, haptics and volume choices.Local preferences for the current app install.
Full-game unlock statusRemember whether the one-time purchase that unlocks the remaining levels is active.Local; kept when you reset progress. Restore it through the store after reinstalling.

In-app purchase

Levels 1–3 are free. The remaining levels unlock with one optional, one-time in-app purchase. Google Play (Android) or the Apple App Store (iOS) processes that purchase, and your payment and billing details, under its own terms and privacy policy (Google, Apple). The maintainers do not receive your card or billing details; like any app publisher, they may receive the sales and transaction reports each store provides.

The app asks the store for the product and its price, confirms a completed purchase, and saves the unlock on your device. Restore purchase asks the store for purchases already made with your store account.

A platform backup or restore may retain preferences. The maintainers cannot promise that uninstalling the app erases every backup copy; platform behavior controls that.

03 / Around the website

Two public surfaces,
different rules.

Landing website

This static site is hosted on Netlify. Netlify may process IP address and request information under its own privacy policy when serving a page. This site adds no analytics, advertising, cookies, third-party trackers or automatic gameplay collection.

GitHub support issues

Choosing a support form takes you to a public issue in the Scrap Trap website repository. GitHub requires an account to submit it. The issue may show your GitHub username, attachments and anything you write; GitHub controls that service under its own privacy statement. Do not put passwords, personal data, identifiers or sensitive logs in a public issue.

The maintainers use information you choose to submit to investigate problems, respond to questions and consider improvements. Issues and comments may remain publicly available after they are closed. You can edit or delete your own comments using GitHub’s controls; contact GitHub through its privacy statement for questions about data it holds.

04 / Your choices

Reset what is yours
to reset.

  • Use the game’s progress-reset control when you want to clear local progress. Settings are designed to remain.
  • Resetting progress keeps the full-game unlock. After reinstalling or changing devices, use Restore purchase in the game to recover it from your store account.
  • Do not submit a support issue if the details cannot be public; redact sensitive logs instead.
  • For a GitHub issue, use GitHub’s own account and privacy controls for requests about your username or repository content.
  • There is no Scrap Trap gameplay account, mailing list or email-based support intake on this site.

05 / Questions

Questions about
your data?

Scrap Trap is owned and published by Luong Nguyen, who is responsible for this policy. For questions about this policy or the game’s data handling, contact Luong Nguyen using the feedback form on Scrap Trap Support. This is a public channel: keep your question general and do not include personal or sensitive information.
